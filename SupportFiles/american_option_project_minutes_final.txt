5. FINAL METHODOLOGICAL STORYLINE
================================================================================

Longstaff–Schwartz:
- Regresses discounted realized future cash flows on functions of current state.
- Main practical empirical algorithm.
- More directly tied to stopping-rule construction.
- In the project results, it is more sensitive to validation design and approximation design.

TVR:
- Regresses discounted next-step estimated values / fitted value iteration.
- Main matched comparator.
- More clearly approximate-dynamic-programming in interpretation.
- In the project results, it is more validation-stable but often mildly conservative in benchmark alignment.

Core comparison takeaway:
- The two methods solve the same optimal-stopping problem under the same shared testbeds, but they do not behave identically numerically.
- Longstaff is the more direct and practical continuation-regression method, but more sensitive to fit/eval separation and design choices.
- TVR is more stable under out-of-sample evaluation, but often behaves as the more conservative fitted approximation.

- Spot grid S0 in {36, 38, 40, 42, 44}

  - path sensitivity
  - exercise-grid sensitivity
  - basis sensitivity
  - seed sensitivity

TVR vanilla:

- Robustness studies include:
  - in-sample vs out-of-sample

Main vanilla findings:
